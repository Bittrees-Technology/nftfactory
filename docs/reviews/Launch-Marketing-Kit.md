# NFTFactory launch marketing kit

Status: prepared for use after contract, backend, wallet and public deployment acceptance. No outreach has been sent. No analytics vendor, tracking cookie, or paid service has been added. Keep the $0/month budget.

## Positioning

NFTFactory is a creative workshop for independent artists: make artwork collectible, organize a body of work, and customize a creator page. Lead with creative control and a clear publishing flow. Avoid investment returns, guaranteed permanence, free minting, copyright-transfer implications, or unsupported network claims.

## First cohort

Start with three to five artists who already want to publish work. The owner supplies recipient details and approves sending. Ask each artist to complete the walkthrough, try profile customization and an existing-collection import, and report confusing steps. Obtain explicit permission before quoting feedback, featuring images, or publishing their names. Do not copy test profiles into promotional material.

## Invitation draft

Hi [name], we’re preparing NFTFactory, a workshop for publishing NFT artwork and building a customizable creator page. Would you like to try the workflow with your own work and tell us what feels clear or confusing? We’ll begin on a test network and explain the wallet steps and storage choices. There’s no obligation to publish publicly or be featured. If you’re interested, we’ll send the walkthrough and test-session details.

## Launch post draft

Make it yours. Make it collectible. NFTFactory brings NFT creation, collections, and a customizable creator page into one workshop. Start with the creator walkthrough, review every wallet request, and publish when you’re ready. Explore the supported networks and current service availability at nftfactory.org.

Publish only after the public release is verified; replace the final destination with the canonical live walkthrough link. Do not announce Base, Robinhood, or mainnet support before those networks are deployed and tested.

## Free measurement plan

For the first cohort, use voluntary feedback and a manual aggregate funnel. Count participants at each stage: Create opened, preview reached, receipt confirmed, import completed, public profile saved. Do not collect wallet addresses, email addresses, NFT identifiers, private tags, or transaction hashes in the marketing sheet. Keep operational logs separate.

Record weekly totals in launch-funnel.csv. One participant may complete several actions; conversions are stage totals, not revenue. Leave counts blank until observed. Keep qualitative notes anonymous and consensual. After two weeks, fix the largest observed drop-off and repeat; do not claim conversion uplift without a baseline.

Before adding automatic analytics, settle the privacy notice, consent/opt-out behavior, retention, and owner access. This is deliberately not a silent tracking deployment.
